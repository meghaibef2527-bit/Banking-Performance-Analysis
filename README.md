# Banking-Performance-Analysis
Average GNPA Ratio by Sector
This analysis compares the quarterly average Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) ratio across Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) and private sector banks from 2021 to 2025.
![Banking Sector Financial Performance Dashboard](Banking%20Sector%20Financial%20Performance%20Dashboard.png)

Key observations

PSU banks show consistently higher GNPA ratios than private sector banks throughout the period.

Both sectors show a declining trend in GNPA ratios, suggesting an improvement in reported asset quality over the period shown.

The gap between the two sectors narrows over time, although PSU banks continue to report higher ratios.

Tools: Python · Data Analysis · Data Visualisation

## Results & Key Findings

The quarterly analysis compares the average Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) ratio of Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) banks and private sector banks from 2021 to 2025.

- **Higher GNPA in PSU banks:** PSU banks consistently reported higher average GNPA ratios than private sector banks throughout the period analysed.
- **Declining GNPA trend:** Both banking sectors showed a downward trend in average GNPA ratios, indicating an improvement in reported asset quality over the period.
- **Persistent sectoral gap:** Although the difference between the two sectors narrowed over time, PSU banks continued to report higher GNPA ratios.
- **Comparative performance:** Private sector banks maintained lower GNPA ratios throughout the observation period, reflecting comparatively lower reported levels of stressed assets in this analysis.

## Business Decisions & Recommendations

Based on the observed trends, the following actions could support banking-sector performance monitoring:

1. **Strengthen credit risk monitoring:** Banks with higher GNPA ratios should review loan portfolios and monitor potentially stressed accounts more closely.
2. **Improve recovery strategies:** Evaluate recovery processes, collection efficiency and resolution mechanisms for non-performing assets.
3. **Monitor sectoral differences:** Use quarterly comparisons to identify changes in the asset-quality gap between PSU and private sector banks.
4. **Track trends over time:** Monitor GNPA alongside additional indicators such as Net NPA, provision coverage ratio and credit growth to develop a more comprehensive assessment.
5. **Use data-driven reporting:** Develop a recurring dashboard to track asset-quality indicators and identify periods requiring further investigation.

## Limitations

- The findings are based on the dataset and aggregation method used in this project.
- The analysis identifies trends and associations; it does not establish the causes of changes in GNPA.
- The GNPA ratio alone is insufficient to determine the overall financial health or profitability of a bank.
- Findings should be validated against the original data source and bank-level observations before being used for investment or policy decisions.
